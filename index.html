Groundline — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 August 2026

Groundline is a radio coverage mapping tool for volunteer firefighters. Members record how well their radio works at a given place; the app builds a coverage map from those readings.

What Groundline collects

When you tap a rating in the app, one reading is created and sent to the brigade's server:

DataDetail
GPS coordinatesLatitude and longitude at the moment you rate
GPS accuracyThe device's own estimate, in metres. Readings worse than 50 m are rejected
Radio quality ratingYour 1–5 rating
TimestampWhen the reading was taken
Device identifierA random ID generated on first launch. Not your name, phone number, or account

Nothing else leaves the device.

What Groundline does not collect

  • Your name, phone number, email address, or any brigade membership record
  • Your contacts, photos, files, or messages
  • Your location in the background. Groundline only reads location while the app is open and only when you take a reading. It cannot track your movements while closed
  • Advertising identifiers. There is no advertising and no analytics SDK in the app
  • Anything at all if you never submit a reading

Why it is collected

To map radio coverage across the brigade response area, so members know where their radio will and will not work before they need it. There is no other purpose. The data is not sold, rented, or used for advertising.

Who can see it

  • Brigade administrators can see all readings, including the device identifier, through an access-controlled operations portal. The identifier is there so an administrator can spot a faulty device sending bad data — it does not tell them who you are
  • The coverage map in the app shows readings without any device identifier attached
  • No one else. Readings are not shared with any third party, government body, or commercial partner

Offline readings

If you have no signal, readings are stored on your phone and sent when connectivity returns. While stored, they sit in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. Groundline excludes this storage from Google and Apple device backups, so unsent readings are not copied to your personal cloud account or transferred to a new phone.

Where the data is stored

On Amazon Web Services in the Sydney region (ap-southeast-2). It is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS between your phone and the server.

How long it is kept

Readings are kept indefinitely, as brigade operational records. Coverage does not expire — a reading taken two years ago still describes that hilltop — so there is no automatic deletion.

If you want your readings removed, contact the brigade at the address below with the approximate dates and area your readings cover. An administrator will locate and delete them. Because readings carry no name — only a random device identifier — this is the only way to match readings to the person who took them.

Other services the app contacts

Two services are contacted directly by your phone. No readings are sent to either.

  • CARTO supplies the background map tiles. It receives your IP address and which map tiles you are viewing
  • OpenStreetMap Nominatim handles the address search box. It receives your IP address and the text you type into the search field, and only when you use the search

Permissions the app requests

Location (while in use). Required. Without it there is nothing to record a reading against. You can revoke it in your device settings at any time; the app will then be unable to log readings.

Children

Groundline is intended for members of a volunteer fire brigade and is not directed at children.
