Groundline

Radio coverage mapping for volunteer fire brigades.

Brigade members rate how well their radio, water supply or vehicle access works at a place. Groundline turns those ratings into a shared map, so members know where their radio will and will not work before they need it.

Getting started

  • Install Groundline and open it.
  • Tap Scan QR code and scan the QR code from your brigade's invite email, or type or paste the invite code instead.
  • Allow location access while using the app. Every reading is tied to where you are standing.

Common questions

What if I have no signal? Readings are saved on your phone and sent automatically when you are back in coverage.

Why was my reading rejected? Readings are only accepted when your GPS fix is accurate to within 50 metres. Move into the open and wait for a better fix.

My invite code stopped working. Your brigade has issued a new one. Ask your brigade administrator.

Can I delete my readings? Yes. Email us with the approximate dates and area of your readings. See the privacy policy for details.
